# The Camnex Security Manifesto

We believe that personal and commercial security systems must belong exclusively to their owners:
1. **Your Video is Your Property**: No third-party corporation should hold your security footage hostage behind monthly recurring fees.
2. **Local First, Always**: Even if the entire internet goes down, your cameras must record unhindered with 100% fidelity.
3. **True Hardware Quality**: Real Sony optical sensors, solid aluminum IP67 casings, and dedicated neural hardware chips.
