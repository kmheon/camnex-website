## CamneX Property Solutions Section

A solution-first property discovery section designed for CamneX Bangladesh, allowing visitors to instantly find complete security, networking, and smart technology packages tailored to their specific environment.

### Purpose & Architecture
* **Solution-First Philosophy**: Bridges the gap between raw product browsing and environment-specific packages across 10 distinct properties (*Home, Apartment, Office, Retail Shop, Restaurant, Warehouse, Factory, Educational Institute, Hospital, Hotel*).
* **Modular Structure**: Located in `frontend/features/property-solutions/` adhering strictly to vanilla HTML, CSS, and JS modular practices.

### Responsive Behavior
* **Desktop**: Modern 12-column Bento Grid featuring large asymmetrical hero cards and 3-column compact cards.
* **Tablet**: Fluid re-flow into full-width large/medium cards and 2-column compact layouts.
* **Mobile**: Smooth horizontal snap-scrolling card carousel maintaining generous card dimensions and touch targets.

### Animations & Micro-Interactions
* Card elevation lift (`translateY(-6px)`) paired with heightened shadow glow (`0 35px 65px`).
* Immersive image zoom (`scale(1.06)`) on hover with smooth gradient shifts.
* High-weight "Explore Solution" CTA featuring smooth color transitions and animated arrow translation (`translateX(6px)`).
